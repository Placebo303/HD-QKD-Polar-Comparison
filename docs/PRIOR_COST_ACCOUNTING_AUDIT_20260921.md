## 1. C1 — what the frozen accounting DOES count (quoted)

Authoritative λ_total contract (`docs/ROADMAP-20260921.md:229-231`):

> - **会计**：per-source 分解、`undetected` 严格隔离（绝不并入 success/FER）、
>   披露按最终前缀、`beta` 只推导、`f_super`/`f_eff` 双数并列、
>   verification-aware `λ_total = leak_EC + 64`（≡ ε_EV ≈ 2⁻⁶³）。

Frozen f/net convention (`PROGRAM_PLAN.md:58-60,34,68-69`):

> 冻结选择（唯一口径）：**整帧含 tag f，帧组为 n=1024 超帧（4×256，既有帧格式不变，单 64-bit tag 摊薄）**。
> 算术规则：单帧泄漏 = 5·m_total + 64（GF(32)² 分层臂，每行 5 bits）或 10·m + 64（q=1024 直接臂）；
> 超帧泄漏 = 4×单帧 syndrome + 64；分母 content = n·H_full（超帧 n=1024）。
> ### 1.2 净收益账（n-based canonical：net = A·5·n − leak，A=已接受帧数；冻结口径含 tag，见 §1.3）
> 净收益 canonical（net=A·5·n−leak）：R3 1280−2550=−1270；binary 1280−651=+629；
> NB nominal 超帧 5120−1064.96=+4055.04（帧等效 +1013.76）。

## 6. The ~1.8× ratio: arithmetically supported, and under what assumptions

No repo-grounded computation yields 1.8× as a *prior-cost* ratio. Repo-derivable neighbours:

- (a) 262144/138516 ≈ **1.893** (CAL symbols ÷ claimed key bits) — near 1.9× but the dividend is absent from the repo, so this is a coincidence, not evidence.

On current evidence (b) suggests the figure, if real, measures unit confusion.

## 8. Honest severity assessment

**Moderate, not critical — but publication-blocking until positioned.** For: the gap is real and total (C1 CONFIRMED — λ_total has exactly two terms, and composable finite-size is punted to a companion paper, so no downstream document will catch it). Against critical: (i) under the concern's own 45.8-kb figure the correction is ~4.5% at CAL scale and ~0.5% at full-pool reuse — material for headline SKR numbers, not order-of-magnitude; (ii) current V80 claims are synthetic-gate claims (FER/leakage at fixed m), where the prior is a fixed read-only bundle and the exposure is nil; (iii) the repo already lists sibling uncounted items (blind rounds, L1 share), so the natural disposition is "third item on the known-extras list," not a refutation of the line. The one asymmetry that keeps severity above low: failed-frame economics and the tag are counted *per attempt*, while the prior — the single largest undisclosed input (262k CAL symbols / 560k TRAIN pairs) — has no stated amortization basis at all.

**Single piece of evidence that would most change severity:** a frozen reuse/amortization statement for the TRAIN/CAL prior — one-time factory calibration reused across the full Jan-21 pool (→ ~0.5%, severity → low) versus per-block/per-session re-estimation disclosed against that block's key (→ potentially fatal at n=1024 scale, severity → critical). Second, unrelated but decisive for C3: producing the actual "P20Q 138,516 ÷ 32,768" source document — its absence is currently the audit's hardest finding.
