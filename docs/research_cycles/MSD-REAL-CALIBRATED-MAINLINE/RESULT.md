# P1 — conditional information budget

The saved R1 TRAIN distributions support continuing an MSD prototype with natural labels, LSB-first order and a long block; this is a planning recommendation, not accepted decoder performance.

The script-generated [table](P1_TABLE.md) and [full-precision numbers](P1_NUMBERS.json) compare complete Bob side information and decoded-prefix conditioning against marginal bit-error BSC costs. Both complete orders close to the same empirical H(A|B). The distinction matters: the BSC proxy overcount is not itself a measured f improvement. Natural labeling and order also change per-plane dispersion and the modeled finite-length allocation.

For the explicitly assumed failure and tag cadence in the table, natural LSB-first scenarios enter the historical disclosure projection at both block lengths; the short-block expected-cost margin is thin. Gray scenarios at the shorter block exceed the projection, while the longer block has room. This does not close either block-length or encoding route: same-source practical code gap, early-error propagation and OOS FER remain unmeasured. The long-block budget is an EC-density projection with a shared tag, not an observed long-block baseline.

Units and ledger: H and plane H are bits/symbol; information-density variances are bits squared; L_EC, tag, failure penalty and Y are bits/block; f is dimensionless. Let kept=N H(A)-L_EC, Y=kept-tag-kept p_fail, and f=(L_EC+tag+kept p_fail)/(N H(A|B)). P1 p_fail is an assumption, not validation data. Nominal slack compares L_EC+tag; expected-numerator slack also includes failure penalty. Empirical unsmoothed counts are descriptive TRAIN estimates, without a new CI or fresh holdout claim.

Input limitation and correction: CQ roots saved summaries only. P1 therefore uses the three previously accepted R1 TRAIN sparse joint-count inputs, preserving their natural time-bin axes and TRAIN role. It does not report CQ, VAL/HOLD or new raw statistics. Exact paths, encoding, orders, commands, assumptions and ceilings are frozen in [PREREG_AND_AUTH](PREREG_AND_AUTH.md).
