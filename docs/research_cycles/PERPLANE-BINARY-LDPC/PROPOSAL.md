## §1 The hypothesis, stated so it can fail

**H1 (one sentence)**: On the measured channel — `ser` 0.2386–0.2543 with zero observed cross-plane co-error and single-plane-flip structure (co-error off-diagonals exactly 0.0, Gray popcount mass confined to {0, 1}, `expected_planes_flipped_per_error` 1.0 both routes; about a million pairs with zero double-plane errors exclude only multi-plane rates above roughly 1e-6, so rare doubles below the detection floor are not excluded), a monotone MSB→LSB ladder spanning roughly three orders of magnitude, ~50% of errors on the LSB plane, and almost entirely asymmetric ±1-bin shifts — nine-to-ten per-plane rate-adapted binary LDPC codes (one code per Gray plane at that plane's measured rate) decoded with a qualified asymmetry-aware decoder achieve strictly lower frame error than the GF(32) nonbinary mainline on the **same** eval superframes at **no larger** nominal disclosure, with the margin stated below.

> **Plane-count discrepancy (recorded, not silently fixed)**: the tasking states "nine Gray plane components"; every machine artifact shows **ten**: `BITS = 10` (`nonbinary_v25_gate.py:28`), a 10×10 co-error matrix (`:294-299`), and 10-element `plane_rates_lsb_first` vectors in all five CQ JSONs (e.g. `workspace/cq_4af91a87/ArmB/CQ-J21a/CQ-J21a.json:182-193`). This proposal uses **ten** (the frozen code and artifact value) and records the "nine" as a tasking-text discrepancy. If a future packet intends a 9-plane treatment (e.g. excluding a degenerate plane), it must freeze which plane and why; nothing here assumes it.

**Baseline (measured, frozen)**: the GF(32) mainline decodes the real 2M frames but at roughly 4% frame error — 2M m=208: 16 fails of 383 superframes, FER 0.041775 [0.025875, 0.066776] (`M0-REALFRAME/RESULT.md` §2.3) — with a minimum measured `f_eff` of 1.464047 on the 1p5M m=207 arm (`M0-REALFRAME/INDEPENDENT_ACCEPTANCE.md` re-review PASS list; `M0-REALFRAME/RESULT.md` §2.2 table). The minimum-`f_eff` figure is cited as the NB mainline's own recorded number; it is **not** a target to reproduce for the binary method (§4 forbids `f_eff` for these methods).

**Success threshold (frozen here, applied only in a future DECIDE Stage 2)**: on the identical 2M eval superframes (383 frames, same `(a,b)`, same provenance per §4), at nominal disclosure **no larger** than the M0 m=208 budget (5·208+64 = 1104 bits/superframe including the single 64-bit tag; the M0/V80 frozen comparison basis per `m2real-accounting-correction/design.md` §0), the per-plane scheme achieves a point FER **at most half** the baseline point estimate — i.e. ≤ 8/383 (≈ 0.0209) — **and** its 95% Wilson upper bound lies strictly below the baseline point estimate 0.0418. Both conditions must hold on the same frames with the same accounting (§4); actual disclosure is ledgered separately and must not exceed the nominal budget either.

**What falsifies H1**: any of (a) Stage-0 achievable-rate arithmetic shows the per-plane budget already exceeds the nominal disclosure at the target FER even under ideal per-plane coding (kill without decoding); (b) Stage-2 measured FER point estimate exceeds 0.0209 or its Wilson interval overlaps/crosses 0.0418 at ≤ nominal disclosure; (c) measured actual disclosure exceeds the nominal budget at the achieved FER; (d) the asymmetry-aware decoder shows no FER gain over the corrected symmetric decoder at matched disclosure (the asymmetry sub-claim fails even if the rate sub-claim survives — record separately, do not pool).

**Scope limits (binding on any citation)**: a win is a win **on this channel at this error rate** (`ser` ≈ 0.24–0.25, 200 ps bins, `dimension = 1024`, Gray mapping, the five measured captures) and **at the tested block length** (§2.3). It is not a general claim about high-dimensional reconciliation, about binary vs nonbinary families, about other error rates, bin widths, dimensions, mappings, or longer blocks. The measured channel's independence may itself be bin-width-conditioned (§6, risk R3); the proposal's claim ceiling is the CHAN-QUALITY-SURVEY ceiling carried forward: channel numbers here establish no FER, efficiency, leakage, SKR, or method ranking until a DECIDE Stage 2 measures them.

## §2 Design — decisions to be made, not a shopping list

### 2.1 Code construction per plane

**Decision D-C1 (frozen here as a requirement; the choice itself belongs to the Stage-0/1 packet)**: one binary LDPC code per Gray plane (ten codes), each at that plane's measured rate — never a uniform per-plane rate, never a pooled cross-source rate.

- **Rate per plane, and how it is chosen**: `R_k = 1 − f_target · h2(p_k)` with `p_k` the plane's **own-source measured** error rate from the persisted CQ JSONs (per-source vectors, e.g. `CQ-J21a.json:182-193`; never the V17 reference ladder, never a cross-source average — cross-source reuse is refused by M2 precedent, `m2lb_arm_runner.py:829-850`). `f_target` is the single efficiency knob frozen per stage (Stage 0 proposes `f_target = 1.3` to match the frozen gate, then reports sensitivity at 1.2/1.4 without tuning). `m_k = ceil(n_plane · (1 − R_k))` disclosed bits per plane; `Σ m_k` plus the tag is the nominal disclosure compared against the 1104-bit budget. Planes with `m_k ≤ 0` or `m_k > n_plane` are recorded infeasible with reason (cf. the dimension-probe feasibility discipline, `EXPLORATION_LOG.md` Entry 6); infeasibility of any plane at `f_target` is a Stage-0 kill input, not a clamping license.
- **Binary density-evolution route — stated plainly**: the repo's V26 Monte-Carlo DE kernel (`nonbinary_v26_mcde.py:1-18,243-326`) is **nonbinary** — GF(2)/GF(32)/GF(512) posterior populations, GF edge-coefficient permutation (`build_gf_perm_table`, `_check_update_coeff_jit`), WHT/XOR convolution, bits/symbol entropy observable. It **cannot be adapted by parameter change** to binary-LDPC threshold prediction; reuse would be a rewrite of the channel sampler, the check update (binary XOR instead of GF-permuted WHT), and the entropy observable. The proposal therefore requires **either** (i) a dedicated binary DE route (BSC with per-plane `p_k`, binary message-passing Monte-Carlo or discretized DE, frozen before use), **or** (ii) rate-targeted PEG search with empirical screening on the measured channel, **with the choice frozen in the Stage-0/1 packet**. The recommended default is (ii) first: PEG construction per plane at the `m_k` targets with the frozen gate family already in repo (`construct_plane_production` + twice-identical support + `fc=0` + binary-support GF(2) rank ∈ {m_j−1, m_j}, `m2lb_arm_runner.py:713-826`), because it reuses reviewed machinery and produces testable codes without a new DE theory dependency. Binary DE becomes required only if Stage-1 screening shows threshold-limited failure that search alone cannot diagnose (escalate as DECIDE design change; cf. dimension-probe §7.4 dv=3 threshold precedent and §7.9–7.11 allocation controls).
- **Why rate-targeted search is honest here**: the dimension probe already measured, on synthetic ladder channels at n=256/dv=3, that per-plane failure is monotone in the plane's code rate with `undetected ≈ 0` and that entropy-proportional allocation beats levelling (FINDINGS §§7.4, 7.9–7.11) — but also that native GF(q) beat per-plane binary by 50.8–558× **at SER ≈ 0.067–0.077 on short blocks with a different decoder bundle** (tanh-SPA vs FFT-QSPA; §§7.6, 7.14–7.15). Those ratios do **not** transfer to `ser` ≈ 0.25 and do not license a construction choice here; they license only the mechanism to watch (finite-length plane-rate binding) and the qualifier to carry (decoder-bundle confound).

### 2.2 Block length — what is used and why, with the literature stated honestly

- **Used**: `n_plane = 1024` bits per plane (one 1024-symbol superframe yields one 1024-bit block per plane). Reason: it is the **only length that preserves same-frame comparability** with the M0 baseline (205/287/383 superframes of 1024 symbols; `M0-REALFRAME/RESULT.md` §§2.1–2.3). Any concatenation across superframes (2048/4096-bit plane blocks) changes the science input (frame definition, tag amortization, error correlation across superframes) and requires its own DECIDE accounting change — explicitly deferred to post-Stage-2, never smuggled into Stage 2.
- **Honesty about literature dependence on long blocks**: Müller's binary results sit at `n = 2^16 = 65536` bits (`LITERATURE_DIRECTION_MEMO_20260921.md:11-13`); Zhou's AIR polar results span up to `n = 2^30` with the tag-inclusive convention, and their own finite-length scaling runs 1.190@2^16 → 1.046@2^30 (`:20-21` and §3). Our plane block (1024 bits) is **64× shorter** than Müller's and ~10^6× shorter than Zhou's best point. Zhou's curve extrapolates the wrong direction for us (longer is better; we are shorter), and no cited paper reports any `f` at our noise (`H_full = 0.8326` bits/symbol, entropy-equivalent ≈ 0.26, `LITERATURE_DIRECTION_MEMO:43`) or alphabet. The proposal therefore states: **short-block penalty is expected, unquantified, and a first-order kill risk** (§6 R1). Stage 0 must include an explicit finite-length backoff per plane (e.g. report `m_k` at ideal `h2` plus at +10%/+20% backoff); if the LSB plane is infeasible even before backoff, H1 dies in Stage 0.

### 2.3 The LSB problem, stated quantitatively

- Measured LSB rates (LSB-first index 0): CQ-J21a 0.11992, CQ-J21b 0.12777, CQ-J21c 0.12712, CQ-20a 0.11966, CQ-20b 0.12447 (`RESULT.md` §§2–3). At `p ≈ 0.127`, `h2(p) ≈ 0.55` bits (Stage 0 recomputes exactly; do not carry this approximation as a result), so the LSB plane alone demands over half its block in redundancy at `f = 1.3` — i.e. a rate ≈ 0.28–0.35 code at n=1024 under a regular ensemble near or beyond its threshold (cf. dimension-probe §7.4: planes at rate 0.77–0.92 already fail 4–18% at n=256/dv=3; our LSB rate is far lower but our noise far higher — neither precedent transfers, both warn).
- Handling (frozen requirement): the LSB plane gets **its own construction instance and its own redundancy** (no borrowing from quiet planes — borrowing is the levelling direction already measured worse, FINDINGS §7.9); irregular PEG with concentrated check distribution (`make_rho`/`concentrated_check_distribution` precedent, `nonbinary_v26_mcde.py:236-240`) rather than frozen dv=3 regular; and an explicit Stage-0 feasibility gate per plane. If the LSB plane cannot be constructed at `m_0 ≤ 1024` with `fc=0` and rank gates passing, Stage 0 returns KILL with the `m_0` arithmetic as evidence — that is the cheap death H1 earns if it is wrong.

### 2.4 Decoder — where the previous baseline failed, and what replaces it

**What failed**: the real Layered-Binary arms corrected 36 of 28,000 blocks with block FER 0.9963–0.9995 (`design.md` §14). Their backend identity is **structurally unrecordable**: `m2real_runner.py:878-880` calls `spa_decode_with_explicit_fallback(...)` without a sidecar; `m2lb_arm_runner.py:1514,1525-1530` skips the sidecar write when `sidecar=None`; the word `sidecar` occurs zero times in `m2real_runner.py` (`BACKEND_TRACE_REPORT.md` §1). Selection was env-determined (`resolve_spa_decode_fn`, `m2lb_arm_runner.py:1482-1504`: `ldpc` present → `spa_decode_production`; absent → `_spa_decode_numpy_planes` with `BACKEND_ID = "numpy-minsum-fallback (assumed, 非ldpc.BpOsdDecoder)"`, `binary_spa_numpy.py:22-23`). Both paths share the same leakage mapping, status flags, double-gate semantics, and the same `_P_ASSUMED = 0.02` (`m2lb_arm_runner.py:463,1547,1560,1782-1783`; `binary_spa_numpy.py:32-37`), and every real artifact shows `messages_actual = 3.14` and `leak_ec_bits = m` on every block including all 36 successes (`BACKEND_TRACE_REPORT.md` §3). The likely proximate cause of the 0.13% is therefore **not mysterious**: a single uniform BSC prior `p = 0.02` (`LLR0 = log((1−p)/p)`, `binary_spa_numpy.py:80`) applied to ten planes whose measured rates span three orders of magnitude (≈ 0.0002 to ≈ 0.128) mismatches every plane — disastrously overconfident on the LSB plane, wastefully underconfident on the quiet planes.

**How the measured rates replace it (frozen requirement)**: every future binary decode SHALL initialize per-plane channel LLRs from that plane's frozen measured rate `p_k` (own-source, stage-frozen, never refit mid-run): `LLR0_k = log((1−p_k)/p_k)` with the existing clamp interval `[_P_MIN, _P_MAX] = [1e-4, 0.49]` (`binary_spa_numpy.py:26`) retained and reported. The uniform `_P_ASSUMED = 0.02` SHALL NOT appear in any new per-plane decode path except as the retained historical label for the void baseline. Any new run that reuses a uniform prior must refuse (fail closed) under its own packet's machine gate.

## §6 Risks, stated plainly, each with its cheapest test

- **R1 — the per-plane block (1024 bits) is too short for a useful binary LDPC at LSB `p ≈ 0.127`.** A rate ≈ 0.3 irregular code at n=1024 under iterative decoding may sit below its threshold/finite-length waterfall regardless of priors. *Cheapest test*: Stage 0 itself — the `m_k` + backoff table plus a Stage-1 synthetic LSB-only decode at the frozen `m_0` (one plane, 1200 trials, EXPLORE). If the LSB plane alone fails at matched disclosure while quiet planes pass, H1's binding constraint is identified without any real-frame cost.
- **R2 — `BpOsdDecoder` may be too slow for the throughput leg (tasking cites a 30× gap).** The 30× figure is **unverified here** (no throughput artifact cited in the tasking; the only recorded timing signal — 0.078–0.093 s per successful LB block — is explicitly refused as evidence in `BACKEND_TRACE_REPORT.md` §9). The numpy fallback's Python `for` loops over rows × planes × iterations (`binary_spa_numpy.py:92-112`) are themselves slow. *Cheapest test*: Stage-1 wall/iteration ledger on synthetic frames for (a) vs a timed `BpOsdDecoder` install check on one frozen plane (implementation-only timing, EXPLORE, no scientific claim) — measures the gap instead of carrying it.
- **R3 — the observed single-plane-flip structure (zero observed cross-plane co-error at the §1 detection floor) may be a 200 ps bin-width artifact, not a true channel property.** All five captures use `bin_width_ps = 200`, `frame_bins = 1024` as an imposed convention (survey packet §§4, 6; `RESULT.md` §1); Gray popcount {0,1} + zero off-diagonals + `E[planes|error] = 1.0` are measured **at that binning**, and about a million pairs with zero double-plane errors exclude only multi-plane rates above roughly 1e-6, so rare doubles below the detection floor are not excluded at any binning. A different binning could split or merge ±1 mass across planes. *Cheapest test*: none within this proposal (re-binning is a science-input change requiring its own DECIDE packet with alignment re-derivation); the proposal instead fences the claim to the measured binning (§1 scope) and forbids generalizing beyond it.
- **R4 — nine/ten separate plane decodes may cost more in leakage or interaction than the GF(32) joint code.** Per-plane syndromes sum (`Σ m_k`) without joint compression; plane-by-plane hard decisions forfeit any residual joint gain (bounded at 0.545% on the old SER-0.077 aggregate — dimension-probe F3 — but **unmeasured** at SER 0.25); iterative plane interaction (if added) costs messages/rounds with no frozen accounting. *Cheapest test*: Stage-0 `Σ m_k` vs joint `H(A|B)` coherence column plus Stage-1 disclosure ledger at matched FER — if `Σ m_k` already exceeds the joint nominal budget, the interaction question is moot and H1 dies in Stage 0.
- **R5 — the "measured channel" is a narrow slice (five captures, one binning, one mapping, `ser` 0.24–0.25).** the unmeasured groups supply no evidence of an easier operating point on recorded grounds (two SHG out of scope, 1.12 excluded, two REFUSEs with no SER; `RESULT.md` §§4–5; packet §5), so slice-broadening has no purchase in the available data. *Cheapest test*: report the quality axis alongside every Stage-2 number (survey packet §11 pattern: no favourable-subset promotion without a new packet + uncertainty statement); any "works on easier data" claim needs its own packet and is not part of H1.
