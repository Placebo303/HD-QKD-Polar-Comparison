The next implementation-only construction candidate is H=[A|T], where T is a square lower-bidiagonal accumulator with unit diagonal. Its rows are independent over GF(2) by construction; no dense long-block rank elimination is required. Information columns have an explicit caller-specified degree; greedy balanced placement avoids shared row-pair four-cycles when possible and reports unavoidable fallbacks. This is a repeat/accumulate-family structural candidate, not an optimized near-capacity code. Row count and degree are caller parameters, not automatically inferred from zero TRAIN entropies. Sparse construction and fixed structural tests do not authorize a decoder experiment or accept FER/f. A separately explicit, default-disabled mixed-stage conditioning mechanism fixes exactly zero-probability error variables at zero and sends H's remaining columns and nonzero equations to the selected factory. Under this model, H e=delta reduces exactly to H_active e_active=delta; a removed zero equation with nonzero delta is inconsistent and must stop. Stored CSR zeros are eliminated on a copy before finding structurally zero rows. Returned active errors are scattered back before the unchanged original-H syndrome check. Every original transmitted row is still charged. No-equation positive variables retain MAP choices, not certainty. This mechanism includes the all-fixed case when enabled; the earlier flag retains its own behavior when mixed-stage conditioning is disabled. It makes no OOS or BP-equivalence claim.

Common-volume bound (independent algebra review PASS, planning only): for a valid native ledger let H_i be its Alice entropy in bits, L_i actual EC bits and F_i its safe failure indicator (accepted_wrong also has F_i=1). G_i=L_i+(H_i-L_i)F_i lies in [0,H_i]. At equal aggregate A=sum H_i>0 and B=sum conditional entropy bits>0, D=f_long-f_control=(G_long-G_control+T_long-T_control)/B. For fixed tag totals, c=(T_long-T_control)/B and R=A/B give D in [c-R,c+R], variance<=R^2 by the interval bound. For outcome-dependent nonnegative tag totals bounded by T_long_max/T_control_max, D lies in [(-A-T_control_max)/B,(A+T_long_max)/B], with standard deviation bounded by half that interval width. Across-pair independence remains a separate power assumption; it is not supplied by the algebra. One complete-volume pair has at most K*(1+q) native stage attempts for one long and q short blocks, each with K stages. This is neither measured runtime nor a chosen actual tag protocol; old numerical power results remain limited to their original design.
