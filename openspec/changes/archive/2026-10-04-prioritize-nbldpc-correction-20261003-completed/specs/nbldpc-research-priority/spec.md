# ADDED Requirements — correction priority

## Requirement: prioritize correction and reconciliation efficiency

Future NB-LDPC mainline exploration SHALL prioritize additional exact recovery/failed-frame reduction and disclosure-aware reconciliation efficiency. Lower runtime obtained by sacrificing branches/recovery SHALL NOT be treated as the next mainline success under this user steering. Preserve accepted full-six results as a comparator, with their exact source/batch ceilings.

## Requirement: qualify implementation acceleration

Acceleration SHALL remain secondary and SHALL preserve the scientific algorithm, vectors/validity, selection/ties and disclosure to its tested numerical scope. A language port is an example, not a grant for a new implementation or universal equivalence claim. Each new scientific hypothesis still requires its own frozen packet, bounded execution and independent review.
