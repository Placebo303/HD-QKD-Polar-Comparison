# GF32 train-source mapping

Track DECIDE. The tool SHALL execute only the frozen packet, inspect metadata
and train provenance before count values, and STOP on ambiguous source/split
or axis roles. It SHALL compute per-source natural F03 U1 XOR-error summaries
without pooling, export no full/conditional A/B arrays, preserve old artifacts
and receive independent Pre-EXECUTE/Pre-RESULT reviews. It SHALL NOT fit a
prior, run a decoder or turn descriptive train statistics into qualification.
