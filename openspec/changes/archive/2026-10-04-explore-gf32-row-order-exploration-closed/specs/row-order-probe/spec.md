# ADDED Requirements

## Requirement: Fixed within-degree scheduling diagnostic
The EXPLORE probe SHALL compare natural row order to one frozen reversal
within each actual check-degree class, with simultaneous syndrome permutation,
identical code/prior/decoder settings, new paired seeds, explicit arm bindings
and retained raw-vector evidence. It SHALL follow the frozen packet R1–R6,
budget, no-overwrite, no-rerun and claim ceiling; no route or promotion claim.

### Scenario: Complete paired diagnostic
- WHEN the frozen attempt completes without violations
- THEN independent artifact review SHALL precede main acceptance and any
  consolidated scientific result, and the finite synthetic screen only SHALL
  classify the batch.

### Scenario: Interrupted attempt
- WHEN a call/input/resource/artifact failure occurs
- THEN available rows/vectors and reasons SHALL be retained, next calls SHALL
  stop and complete paired performance totals SHALL remain null.
