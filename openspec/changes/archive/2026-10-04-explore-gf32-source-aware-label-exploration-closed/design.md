# Minimal design

Reuse accepted deep graph, cycle inventory, exact source-overlap and paired
observation helpers. Fixed-support incidence lists restrict each coordinate
evaluation to affected cycles; unique variable supports make per-cycle sum
equal unique-orbit sum. Full-reference numerical checks and final rank52
admission prevent objective drift or changed code dimension. No extra sweep,
rank-preserving optimizer, graph selection or general search framework.
Scientific thresholds, tolerances, inputs and costs are frozen in S1–S5.
