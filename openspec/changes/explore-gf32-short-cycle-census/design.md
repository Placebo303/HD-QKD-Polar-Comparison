# Design

Pure degree2 check-multigraph DFS over shared support once per fixedgraph,
canonical alternating-vertex keys, then exact GFproducts for two frozen
matrices and normalized local unit-cycle codeword witnesses. Preserve
parallel-edge ell2cycles, forbid repeatedvertices. Bounded states/cycles,
partialunknown aggregates, no implicitdecoder. Minimal additive runner/test.
